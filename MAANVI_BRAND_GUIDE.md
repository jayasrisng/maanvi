# MAANVI Brand Guide

Use this file as the source of truth for Maanvi’s visual identity.  
When generating designs, websites, social posts, packaging, presentations, UI, illustrations, or written creative direction for Maanvi, follow these rules unless explicitly overridden.

---

## 1. Brand Character

Maanvi should feel:

- Indian, but not cliché
- Rich, saturated, and high-pigment
- Rooted in pattu silk, temple color, saree contrast, and everyday Indian color language
- Warm, feminine, confident, premium, and contemporary
- International in execution
- Cultural without looking ornamental for the sake of ornament
- Bold in color, clean in layout
- Editorial rather than “ethnic boutique”
- Tactile, crafted, and human

Avoid:

- Generic beige luxury branding
- Muted “earth tones”
- Neon colors
- Excessive gold
- Overly decorative Indian motifs
- Generic festive templates
- Too many colors in one composition
- Script fonts as primary typography
- “Boho India” styling
- Overly corporate blue/gray layouts

## 2. Core Brand Colors

These are the primary Maanvi colors.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Sindoor Orange | `#FF4F00` | Primary hero color |
| Ivory | `#FFFFF0` | Main light background |
| Deep Maroon | `#4F0400` | Main dark anchor |

### Sindoor Orange — `#FF4F00`

Use for:

- Hero backgrounds
- Primary calls to action
- Campaign headlines
- Packaging accents
- Social media frames
- Key highlights
- Signature graphic moments

Character:

- High-energy
- Sacred/fire-like
- Bold
- Modern
- Immediate

Do not dilute it into pastel orange.

### Ivory — `#FFFFF0`

Use for:

- Main website backgrounds
- Product pages
- Editorial layouts
- Packaging base
- Negative space
- Typography backgrounds

Prefer Ivory over pure white where possible.

### Deep Maroon — `#4F0400`

Use for:

- Dark backgrounds
- Headlines on Ivory
- Footer
- Packaging
- Borders
- Secondary dark UI
- Rich contrast against Sindoor Orange

Prefer this over black when a warmer result is appropriate.

## 4. Color Usage Rules

### Default proportion

For most brand layouts:

- 50–65% Ivory
- 20–30% one dominant saturated color
- 10–20% Deep Maroon or another dark anchor
- 5–10% secondary accent

Do not use every palette color at once.

### Best signature combinations

#### Maanvi Core
- Sindoor Orange `#FF4F00`
- Ivory `#FFFFF0`
- Deep Maroon `#4F0400`

#### Orange + Blue
- Sindoor Orange `#FF4F00`
- Ashoka Chakra Blue `#000080`
- Ivory `#FFFFF0`

Use for high-contrast campaigns.

#### Rani + Blue
- Rani Pink `#B80955`
- Ashoka Chakra Blue `#000080`
- Ivory `#FFFFF0`

Use for bold pattu-inspired compositions.

#### Green + Ivory
- Pattu Green `#0C9924`
- Bottle Green `#006B3C`
- Ivory `#FFFFF0`

Use for richer, calmer product stories.

#### Kaapi + Ivory
- Kaapi Brown `#4A160A`
- Ivory `#FFFFF0`
- Sindoor Orange `#FF4F00`

Use for premium/editorial layouts.

#### Kumkum + Mango
- Kumkum Red `#E00425`
- Mango Gold `#F2920C`
- Ivory `#FFFFF0`

Use sparingly for festive or ceremonial work.

## 5. Typography System

Maanvi uses separate but complementary Latin and Telugu systems.

# English / Latin Fonts

## Primary Display — Fraunces

Use for:

- Hero headlines
- Campaign titles
- Collection names
- Editorial titles
- Large packaging copy
- Fashion/editorial statements

Recommended weights:

- Regular
- SemiBold
- Black

Character:

- Sculptural
- Crafted
- Warm
- Editorial
- Distinctive

Use it large.

Avoid using Fraunces for long body copy.

---

## Primary Sans — Manrope

Use for:

- Website UI
- Navigation
- Product information
- Buttons
- Captions
- Labels
- Pricing
- Body copy
- Utility text
- Social media details

Recommended weights:

- Regular
- Medium
- SemiBold
- Bold

For labels and navigation, uppercase Manrope with moderate tracking is preferred.

Example:

`NEW ARRIVALS`

`HANDWOVEN IN INDIA`

`SHOP SILK`

---

## Editorial Accent — Cormorant Garamond Italic

Use sparingly for:

- Collection names
- Poetic phrases
- Product stories
- Pull quotes
- Editorial captions
- Elegant secondary statements

Recommended styles:

- Italic
- SemiBold Italic

Do not use it for UI or long paragraphs.

---

# Telugu Fonts

## Signature Telugu — Lepakshi

This is the primary cultural Telugu display font.

Use for:

- Telugu campaign titles
- Heritage storytelling
- Festival creatives
- Packaging
- Telugu collection names
- Editorial moments
- Cultural brand statements

Lepakshi should feel distinctive and intentional.

Do not use it for dense body copy.

---

## Playful Telugu — Baloo Tammudu 2

Use for:

- Children’s collections
- Storybook content
- Warm family campaigns
- Playful social media
- Illustrated posts
- Friendly labels

Character:

- Rounded
- Familiar
- Playful
- Warm
- Child-friendly

Do not use this font for luxury/editorial campaigns.

---

## Editorial Telugu — Noto Serif Telugu

Use for:

- Longer Telugu reading
- Editorial copy
- Cultural essays
- Product stories
- Printed text where readability matters

---

## Functional Telugu — Noto Sans Telugu

Use for:

- Telugu UI
- Website copy
- Captions
- Navigation
- Product details
- Care instructions
- Utility text

This is the Telugu equivalent of Manrope.

---

## Optional Soft Telugu — Mandali

Use sparingly for:

- Warm secondary headings
- Softer editorial moments
- Informal Telugu copy

## 6. Preferred Font Pairings

### Brand / Campaign
Fraunces + Lepakshi

Use when the design needs personality, heritage, and visual impact.

### Digital / Functional
Manrope + Noto Sans Telugu

Use for websites, apps, UI, labels, product information, and navigation.

### Editorial
Cormorant Garamond Italic + Noto Serif Telugu

Use for softer, literary, or storytelling moments.

### Playful / Children
Manrope + Baloo Tammudu 2

Use for children’s content, illustrated campaigns, and family-oriented storytelling.

## 7. Logo Rules

The Maanvi logo/wordmark should be treated as custom artwork.

Do not:

- Recreate the logo using a standard font
- Stretch it
- Condense it
- Add shadows
- Add gradients
- Add outlines
- Place it on visually noisy backgrounds
- Change proportions

Preferred logo contexts:

- Deep Maroon on Ivory
- Ivory on Sindoor Orange
- Ivory on Deep Maroon
- Sindoor Orange on Ivory

If the illustrated Maanvi character is used, keep the surrounding design cleaner so the character remains the focus.

---

## 8. Layout Direction

Maanvi layouts should feel bold but controlled.

Prefer:

- Large typography
- Strong blocks of color
- Editorial spacing
- Clean margins
- Full-bleed photography
- Pattu-inspired color blocking
- One strong contrast per composition
- Asymmetry when appropriate
- Generous negative space

Avoid:

- Busy borders
- Mandala overload
- Generic paisley decoration
- Gold everywhere
- Tiny decorative motifs
- Excessively symmetrical wedding-card layouts
- Too many typefaces
- Too many accent colors

---

## 9. Photography Direction

Photography should emphasize:

- Silk texture
- Fabric movement
- Rich pigment
- Zari reflection
- Human warmth
- Natural skin
- Draping
- Detail
- Craft
- Movement

Preferred lighting:

- Warm daylight
- Directional light
- Soft shadows
- Slightly cinematic contrast

Avoid:

- Overexposed white catalog photography
- Heavily desaturated edits
- Cool gray fashion grading
- Artificial HDR
- Excessive smoothing

The fabric color should remain dense and believable.

---

## 10. Graphic Language

Maanvi graphics can borrow from:

- Pattu saree contrast borders
- Silk folds
- Temple geometry
- Telugu lettering
- Zari line work
- Textile repetition
- Indian color naming
- Packaging stamps
- Archival textile labels
- Editorial fashion layouts

Use abstraction rather than literal ornament.

Example:
A deep navy block next to vivid orange can evoke a pattu border without drawing a saree motif.

---

## 11. Tone for Generated Visuals

When prompting an image model or design LLM, use language such as:

> High-pigment pattu silk colors, saturated but not neon, dense textile dye, warm Indian editorial fashion, deep contrast, ivory negative space, bold color blocking, refined contemporary composition, tactile silk texture, rich natural light, modern global fashion identity.

Avoid prompts like:

> earthy muted Indian palette

> boho Indian

> pastel ethnic

> traditional festive design

These will usually move away from the Maanvi identity.

## 14. Quick Decision Rules for LLMs

When uncertain:

1. Use Ivory as the background.
2. Use Sindoor Orange as the main accent.
3. Use Deep Maroon instead of black.
4. Use Fraunces for large English headlines.
5. Use Manrope for everything functional.
6. Use Lepakshi for expressive Telugu.
7. Use Noto Sans Telugu for readable Telugu UI/body copy.
8. Use only one or two pattu colors per composition.
9. Keep colors saturated and dense, not pastel.
10. Keep layouts modern and international.
11. Let Indian identity come through color, typography, textile, language, and material—not generic decorative motifs.
12. When in doubt, simplify.

---

## 15. One-Line Brand Direction

**Maanvi is a contemporary Indian visual identity built from the saturated color, texture, contrast, and cultural memory of pattu silk—expressed with modern global restraint.**
