04 / The website

Keep the experience recognisable.

  • Start with the approved original walking-to-seated-logo film, its music and exact mark.
  • On desktop, keep the cloud-and-cotton → silk → Fall 2026 journey.
  • On mobile, use separate image-and-text panels with natural scrolling. Keep text out of image overlays and remove fog effects.
  • Use the Fall 2026 collection after silk. The old campaign portrait is retired.
  • Keep image interpretations clearly labelled; confirm real stock, price and delivery personally.
